AirBoxer Privacy

Updated 2026-05-04 · Data Not Collected

The short version

AirBoxer collects nothing. No accounts. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking. The app runs entirely on your iPhone, reads AirPods motion locally, and never sends any data to a server we control.

What the app uses

  • AirPods motion data. AirBoxer asks for motion permission on first launch. The data (head pitch, roll, yaw at ~25Hz) is read directly from your paired AirPods Pro / Max / 3rd gen via Apple's CMHeadphoneMotionManager API, used in real time to detect dodge gestures, and discarded. It is never stored, transmitted, or analyzed.
  • Game Center (optional). If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple manages your alias and leaderboard scores. Two scores are submitted: longest dodge streak per fight, and total knockouts. No other Game Center data is read or written. You can opt out at any time in iOS Settings → Game Center.
  • Local game state. Your high score, settings preferences (motion sensitivity, sound, haptics, scanline), and tutorial-completion flag are stored on-device via Apple's UserDefaults. They never leave your phone.

What the app does NOT do

  • No account creation or login
  • No advertising or ad SDKs
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No location access
  • No camera or microphone access
  • No contacts, photos, or HealthKit access
  • No third-party services
  • No internet connection required after install (Game Center is optional)

Children

AirBoxer does not collect data from anyone, including children. The game contains cartoon-style boxing animations and is rated 9+ on the App Store for "Infrequent/Mild Cartoon or Fantasy Violence."

Data deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to delete on our end. To remove all local game state from your device, delete the AirBoxer app — iOS will purge all on-device data including settings and high scores. Game Center scores are managed by Apple; visit Game Center settings or contact Apple Support to remove them.

Third-party services

AirBoxer relies only on Apple's first-party frameworks (CoreMotion, GameKit, SpriteKit, SwiftUI, AVFoundation). No third-party SDKs are integrated. Game Center is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If AirBoxer's privacy practices ever change (for example, if a future version adds optional features that need data), this page will be updated and the change date at the top will reflect it. Material changes will be summarized in the App Store "What's New" section of the relevant version.
