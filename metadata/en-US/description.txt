AirBoxer is a head-tracking boxing dodge game.

Pair your AirPods. Tilt your head LEFT or RIGHT to slip a hook. NOD DOWN to duck an uppercut. Don't move into a punch — every opponent telegraphs every swing. The reflex IS the game.


THE OPPONENTS

• SANDBAG — Tutorial. Infinite HP. Verbal callouts on every tell. Learn the rhythm by feel.

• VITRINE BLOOM — Paris. Record 0-99. Slow, telegraphed hooks and a glass jaw. Everyone's first real fight.

• THE WARDEN — Stateville. Record 47-1. Fast telegraphs near the limit of human reaction. The final test.


WHY HEAD TRACKING

AirPods Pro, Max, and 3rd-gen have an H1/H2 motion chip that's been quietly shipping in 100 million ears since 2019. Almost no app uses it as primary input. AirBoxer does — every dodge is your actual head moving in real space, not a tap on a screen. Once you've slipped a hook with your head, taps feel small.


DESIGNED FOR THE RING

• 3 distinct opponents with original characters and original chiptune music
• Per-fight HP, combo streaks, dodge-zone targeting, KO sequences
• Game Center leaderboards: longest dodge streak, total knockouts
• Adjustable motion sensitivity (5 detents) for different necks and play styles
• Pause-on-background so phone calls don't cost you a fight
• Sound + haptics toggles

• Built for iPhone, iOS 16+, with AirPods Pro / Max / 3rd-generation


NO ADS. NO TRACKING. NO IAP.

AirBoxer is a one-time premium purchase. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no analytics. The app collects nothing — your AirPods motion is read locally, used in real time, and discarded. Read the privacy policy at airboxer.app/privacy.


A LOVE LETTER

Inspired by a 1987 NES boxing classic. All characters in AirBoxer are original; the game is not affiliated with or endorsed by any other rights-holder. The slow telegraphed wind-ups of the 1987 era happen to map almost exactly to what AirPods motion can deliver in 2026 — that coincidence is the entire game.

Pair AirPods. Slip the punches. Don't blink.
