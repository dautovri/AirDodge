This app does not require a login or account.

AirBoxer is a head-tracking dodge game using AirPods motion sensors via Apple's CMHeadphoneMotionManager API. To test the app:

1. Pair AirPods Pro, AirPods Max, or AirPods 3rd-generation with the test device. (AirBoxer requires a paired set with the H1/H2 chip — older AirPods do not deliver motion data.)
2. Wear the AirPods.
3. Launch AirBoxer. The app will request Motion permission on first launch — please accept; the app cannot function without it.
4. From the menu, tap FIGHT.
5. The opponent will telegraph each punch with a wind-up animation lasting ~1.6 seconds. During the wind-up:
   - For a LEFT HOOK telegraph (orange warning text reading "DODGE RIGHT →"): tilt your head to the RIGHT.
   - For a RIGHT HOOK telegraph ("← DODGE LEFT"): tilt your head to the LEFT.
   - For a JAB telegraph ("SLIP ASIDE!"): tilt EITHER direction.
   - For an UPPERCUT telegraph ("↓ DUCK!"): nod your head DOWNWARD.
6. Successfully dodge to score points and build a combo. Get hit and lose 1 HP (start with 5).
7. The fight ends in a knockout (KO) when HP reaches 0.

If AirPods are not connected, the on-screen status chip shows "AirPods not detected — demo mode." Without motion input the player cannot dodge — this is expected behavior. Please make sure AirPods are paired before testing the gameplay.

Game Center is OPTIONAL. If signed in, two leaderboard scores are submitted on KO: longest dodge streak and total knockouts. Sign-in is via standard iOS Game Center settings.

The app collects no other data. No analytics. No advertising. No third-party SDKs. Privacy policy: https://airboxer.app/privacy
